How a verdict is decided

  1. The image goes to YOLOv8n, which returns boxes and classes.
  2. Any box at or above the confidence threshold makes the verdict FAIL.
  3. The verdict and an annotated thumbnail are saved to SQLite.

What this does not show

  • The data is small: 320 training and 80 validation images, one split, one seed, no separate test set.
  • Most misses are tiny defects and faint thread errors.
  • These are validation numbers. A real mill needs local data and retraining.

Error review

Validation images the model got wrong. Green is the ground truth and red is the prediction.

What went wrong

  • 15 missed defects and 11 false alarms, out of 101 labelled defects, across 19 images.
  • Thread errors are missed most often, with recall 0.70. Objects and holes have the lowest precision, 0.75 and 0.79.
  • At a threshold of 0.55, precision is 0.97 and recall is 0.64. Misses and false alarms trade off directly.

Not yet proven, and how I would check

  • Thin thread errors may lose detail at 416 px. Retrain at 640 px and compare recall.
  • One small split is not enough. Add a proper train, validation and test split.
  • Validation has no defect-free images, so the real false-alarm rate is unmeasured. Add clean fabric.
