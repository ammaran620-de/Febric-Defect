Pipeline

  1. Image in (upload or webcam frame).
  2. YOLOv8n returns boxes and classes.
  3. Any box at or above the threshold means FAIL.
  4. Verdict and annotated thumbnail go to SQLite.

Limits

  • 320 train / 80 validation images, one split, one seed. No separate test set.
  • Misses are mostly tiny defects and faint thread errors.
  • Numbers are validation-split numbers. A real mill needs local data and retraining.

Errors

Validation images the model got wrong. Green box = ground truth, red = prediction.

What went wrong

  • 15 missed defects and 11 false alarms out of 101 labelled defects, spread over 19 images.
  • Lowest recall: thread error 0.70. Lowest precision: objects 0.75, hole 0.79.
  • Raising the threshold to 0.55 gives precision 0.97 at recall 0.64, so misses and false alarms trade off directly.

Hypotheses and next checks

  • Thin thread errors may lose detail at 416 px. Check: retrain at 640 and compare recall.
  • Small data. Check: a proper train / val / test split.
  • No defect-free images in validation, so the real false-alarm rate is unmeasured. Check: add clean fabric.
